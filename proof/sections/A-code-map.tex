\noindent The test suites cross-check several of these correspondences
empirically. They check:
\begin{itemize}
  \item compiler determinism and translation validation on all 36 timed-automaton
        views of the aligner's corpus;
  \item agreement of enabling windows and invariant deadlines with brute force over
        the tick grid, on randomly generated automata with diagonal constraints;
  \item that replaying a recorded end-to-end mission reproduces every ledger record;
  \item architecture rules: the kernel's dependencies, the single assignment site of
        the authoritative state, and the absence of the twin's location names in
        the proposers.
\end{itemize}
These tests are validation and do not replace the proofs.
